02 / 研究方向Research

研究方向Research themes

从城市群、交通网络到街道，探索空间数据如何支持规划决策。From urban regions and transport networks to individual streets, I use spatial data to inform planning decisions.

01 / Region

京津冀城市群韧性Resilience in Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei

结合多源地理数据与空间建模，评估洪涝风险、识别协同恢复的重点地区，并追踪区域生态韧性的时空演变。I use multi-source geospatial data and spatial modeling to assess flood risk, identify priorities for coordinated recovery, and trace changes in regional ecological resilience.

02 / Network

共享出行与交通公平Shared mobility and transport equity

结合共享单车骑行记录、街景与轨道站域数据，研究建成环境如何影响不同社区的出行机会。I combine bike-share trips, street imagery, and metro catchment data to examine how the built environment shapes travel opportunities across neighborhoods.

阅读共享出行论文Read the shared mobility paper ↗

03 / Street

街景计算与城市感知Street-view analytics and urban perception

用街景影像、遥感指标和可解释建模量化街道环境，并将分析结果用于街道空间评估和更新研究。I combine street-view imagery, satellite-derived indicators, and interpretable models to assess street environments and study their regeneration.
