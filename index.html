01 / Research

Selected research

Three lines of work, from regional resilience to the experience of a single street.

01 / REGION

Urban and regional resilience

Assessing flood risk and coordinated recovery in Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei, alongside research on long-term patterns of ecological resilience.

Ecological resilience paper ↗
02 / MOBILITY

Mobility and spatial equity

Using bike-share records and street-level data to examine how built environments shape shared mobility, including access around metro stations.

Shared mobility paper ↗
03 / STREET

GeoAI and urban perception

Combining street-view imagery and spatial modeling to measure how people perceive streets and to inform planning at the street scale.

Street-view paper ↗
